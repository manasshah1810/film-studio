# Paper cutout: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No outlines | No stroke on any shape; edges come from colour and shadow. | Any outline or keyline. |
| 2 | Scissor edge | Edges visibly irregular; no perfect circle or ruler line. | Clean geometric shapes. |
| 3 | Layer shadow | Every piece casts the same soft shadow in the same direction. | Missing, hard or inconsistent shadows. |
| 4 | Paper texture | Grain reads as paper on every piece without muddying colour. | Flat digital colour or dirty texture. |
| 5 | Ink-strip headline | Headlines sit on slightly rotated ink strips with bg-colour type. | Plain text on the background. |
| 6 | Stop-motion timing | Pieces move on twos, settle, then only the edges boil. | Smooth motion or constant movement. |
| 7 | Set depth | Sun and stepped hills give three clear planes behind the cast. | Empty or single-plane background. |
| 8 | Colour count | Five paper colours or fewer per panel. | Too many colours. |
| 9 | Handmade placement | Small rotations and overlaps; nothing mechanically aligned. | Grid-perfect layout. |
| 10 | Warmth | Overall feel is tactile and friendly; characters read clearly. | Cold or cluttered collage. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
