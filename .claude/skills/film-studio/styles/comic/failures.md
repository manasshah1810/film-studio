# Comic: failure modes

Check these before sending stills to review. Each was seen in a proof render or follows directly from the kit.

1. **Small numbers smear into a blob.** Ink-coloured numerals with an ink outline. The kit already drops the outline for ink; if a versus value still smears, set the side value shorter than 6 characters.
2. **Caption box covers the figure.** Headline over 6 words in a figure panel. Cut it to 2-4 words.
3. **Speed lines everywhere.** Every panel `peak`. Keep one per scene.
4. **Dots turn to grey mush on small exports.** Dot cell is 1.7 u; below 540 px wide it aliases. Proof stills at scale 0.4 or higher.
5. **Looks like a known publisher.** Do not imitate a real trade dress, logo or character. Pick a different palette and pairing if a still reads as a specific title.
6. **Text crosses the border.** Long `sub` on a tight anchor. Shorten or remove the sub; only bubble tails may cross.

## Quick self-check on a still
- None of: gradients, blurs, glows, soft shadows
- None of: fades and slow eases: elements cut in
- None of: more than one panel border style in a film
- None of: speed lines or bursts on more than one panel per scene
- All five signature moves from `style.md` appear somewhere in the film.
