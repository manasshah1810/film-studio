# Comic: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Panel border | Every screen has one inked, slightly off-square panel with a clean paper gutter. | Full-bleed art, missing border, or perfectly square box. |
| 2 | Caption-box headline | Headline sits in a highlight box with ink border and hard shadow, tilted about 1 degree. | Floating headline text or soft shadow. |
| 3 | Ben-Day tone | Shading is dots only, on one side of forms, at one screen angle. | Gradients, or no tone at all. |
| 4 | Ink weight | Heavy uniform brush outline on all figures and props. | Thin or broken outlines. |
| 5 | Burst and speed-line discipline | One burst or speed-line panel per scene, on the peak. | Effects on every panel or none in the film. |
| 6 | Lettering | Balloon text upper case, 4 words or fewer, tail points at the speaker. | Lower-case balloons, long speech, stray tails. |
| 7 | Hard cuts | Elements cut or pop in within 6 frames. | Fades and slow eases. |
| 8 | Page colour | Four-colour feel: paper, ink, two spot colours plus highlight. | Muddy or pastel mixes outside the palette. |
| 9 | Drama staging | Characters face each other; the winner is visually higher or larger. | Flat line-up with no conflict. |
| 10 | Originality | No resemblance to a real character, logo or publisher dress. | Any recognisable borrowed look. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
