# Risograph: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Overprint | Two plates overlap and visibly multiply into a third colour. | Opaque shapes with no overprint. |
| 2 | Misregistration | Display type and shapes carry a consistent hard ghost in one direction. | Perfect registration or blurred shadow. |
| 3 | No outlines | Flat fills only. | Ink keylines. |
| 4 | Ink count | Paper plus four inks or fewer per frame. | Full-colour look. |
| 5 | Halftone | One corner of dot screen at an angle, fading out. | Smooth gradient or none. |
| 6 | Paper grain | Strong grain over everything. | Clean digital flats. |
| 7 | Poster composition | Bold asymmetry, type anchored to an edge, shapes cropped by the frame. | Centred, timid layout. |
| 8 | Type character | Heavy or characterful display face set tight. | Neutral default-looking type. |
| 9 | Legibility on plates | Text over plates stays readable. | Text lost on colour. |
| 10 | On-twos motion | Plates slide in separately, ghost first, held on twos. | Smooth continuous easing. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
