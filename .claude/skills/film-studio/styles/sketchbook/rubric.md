# Sketchbook: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paper presence | Paper colour, rules or dots are visible in every panel. | Flat background with no page. |
| 2 | Line quality | Pencil or pen line with wobble and occasional overdraw. | Clean vector lines. |
| 3 | Handwritten voice | All text is handwritten and reads like notes, not headlines. | Formal sans or serif text. |
| 4 | Marker emphasis | One marker underline or swipe per panel on the key word. | No emphasis, or highlighter everywhere. |
| 5 | Stuck-on elements | Tape, sticky notes or similar appear and sit at slight angles. | Everything perfectly aligned. |
| 6 | Pen economy | One pen colour plus one marker per panel. | Rainbow of colours. |
| 7 | Margin logic | Notes sit in margins and corners like real annotation. | Centred, poster-like layout. |
| 8 | Legibility | Smallest handwriting readable at phone size. | Cramped or thin script. |
| 9 | Intimacy of tone | Panels feel like one person's notes: asides, arrows, corrections. | Reads as a branded template. |
| 10 | Draw-on motion | Lines are drawn; notes are written; nothing slides. | Fades or slides. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
