# Infographic: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grid alignment | Every zone edge, card and band sits on the 4 u grid; nothing tilted. | Elements off-grid or rotated. |
| 2 | Number honesty | Every number has a label, a unit and matches the script exactly; bars start at zero. | Unlabelled figures, truncated axes or changed precision. |
| 3 | Data-ink restraint | One edge band is the only decoration; no outlines, shadows or gradients. | Decorative icons or effects that carry no data. |
| 4 | Two-colour data rule | At most two data colours per chart, used consistently across the film. | Rainbow series or colours that swap meaning. |
| 5 | Count-up craft | Figures count with tabular digits and stop dead, then the underline draws. | Numbers that fade in, wobble or ease past their value. |
| 6 | Card hierarchy | Chart sits on a tinted card with its corner tick; headline is clearly above it. | Chart floating on the grid, or card heavier than the data. |
| 7 | Pictogram neutrality | People are faceless signs in cast colours. | Faces, speech bubbles or cartoon acting. |
| 8 | Reading order | Kicker, figure, label read top-left to bottom in one pass. | Eye has to hunt between competing blocks. |
| 9 | Left-aligned text | All body text is left-aligned to a shared edge. | Centred paragraphs or ragged mixed alignment. |
| 10 | Stillness | Nothing moves once landed; the grid never animates. | Drifting, pulsing or looping decoration. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
