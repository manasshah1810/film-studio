# Infographic: failure modes

Check these before sending stills to review. Each was seen in a proof render or follows directly from the kit.

1. **A number with no label or unit.** Fill `stat.label` and `stat.unit` or `chart.unit`; the validator flags an unlabelled stat.
2. **Chart colours carry no meaning.** Series roles left to default on three series. Keep to two data colours; set `role` explicitly.
3. **Looks like a generic slide deck.** Title panels back to back. Alternate stat, chart, versus and timeline so the card, band and count-up all appear.
4. **Card and band collide.** Anchor on the same side as the edge band with a long label. Shorten the label or let the anchor default.
5. **Invented precision.** Script says "about a third" and the panel shows 33.3%. Show the number exactly as sourced.
6. **Pictograms acting.** Poses like cheer or slump on sign people. Use idle, point or hold only.

## Quick self-check on a still
- None of: outlines on shapes, drop shadows, gradients, 3d charts
- None of: more than two data colours in one chart; the third series is ink
- None of: a number without a label or a label without a unit
- None of: faces, expressions or speech bubbles as jokes
- All five signature moves from `style.md` appear somewhere in the film.
