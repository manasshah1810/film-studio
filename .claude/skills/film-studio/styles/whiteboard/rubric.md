# Whiteboard: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Written, not placed | Every stroke shows start-to-end drawing order and wobble; text has marker pressure variation. | Text or shapes appear whole, slide or fade. |
| 2 | Hatch discipline | All closed shapes are hatched at one angle and gap; no flat fill anywhere. | Any solid fill, or hatch angles that vary. |
| 3 | Board history | Ghost marks of the previous panel are visible but do not compete (about 6%). | Clean board every panel, or ghosts that reduce legibility. |
| 4 | Marker economy | Ink plus at most two marker colours plus one highlighter in the whole film. | A fourth colour, or colour used with no meaning. |
| 5 | Edge-start layout | Content starts from a top or left edge with deliberate empty board. | Centred stacks, or board over 70% covered. |
| 6 | One circled figure | Exactly one number in the film gets the two-loop circle, and it is the key one. | Several circled numbers, or none. |
| 7 | Because-arrows | Arrows connect cause to effect and are drawn after both ends exist. | Decorative arrows, or text and drawing left unconnected. |
| 8 | Hand-lettering legibility | Smallest lettering is readable at phone size; contrast pairs hold. | Thin or small lettering, or highlighter over low-contrast ink. |
| 9 | Write pacing | Each panel holds at least 30 frames after its last stroke lands. | Panels cut while strokes are still drawing. |
| 10 | Teacher presence | The figure, when present, points at or reacts to the content on the board. | A figure that stands beside the content doing nothing. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
