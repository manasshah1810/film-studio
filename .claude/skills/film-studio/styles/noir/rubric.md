# Noir: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Darkness | Background dark with a real fall-off to black at the edges. | Mid-tone or evenly lit frame. |
| 2 | Single light source | Exactly one slatted shaft per scene, readable as light through blinds. | No shaft, several, or stripes covering everything. |
| 3 | Silhouette | Figures are faceless single-tone shapes that separate from the background. | Faces, outlines, or figures lost in the dark. |
| 4 | Rim light | A thin highlight edge on the lit side only. | No rim, or rim all round. |
| 5 | One accent | One accent-coloured element per scene. | Two or more accents. |
| 6 | Typed text | Text types on and stays readable against the shaft. | Fades, or text lost in stripes. |
| 7 | Grain and vignette | Film grain visible and moving; vignette closes the frame. | Clean digital image. |
| 8 | Camera restraint | Slow push only. | Shake, whip or bounce. |
| 9 | Tone of type | Typewriter, didone or condensed gothic; no rounded faces. | Friendly rounded type. |
| 10 | Staging | Subject low or to one side with negative space in the dark. | Centred, evenly spaced composition. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
