# Kinetic type: failure modes

Check these before sending stills to review. Each was seen in a proof render or follows directly from the kit.

1. **More than 9 words on screen.** Split the line across two panels of 1-1.5 s each. The validator counts words per panel.
2. **Type drifts while being read.** A camera or drift effect was added by the VFX plan. Remove it: only the ghost glyph moves.
3. **Box unreadable.** Accent role too close to ink on that palette. Change `accent` to the other accent role.
4. **Ghost glyph reads as a second headline.** Short panel plus high-contrast palette. Keep ghost at 50% and let it crop; do not add a second ghost.
5. **Feels like a lyric video template.** Every panel is a centred title. Alternate anchors (tl, bl, tr) and slab position.
6. **Charts crammed in.** A chart layout in kinetic is plain and weak. Turn the data point into a stat panel.

## Quick self-check on a still
- None of: illustration, texture, gradients, photographs
- None of: more than three colours visible in a panel
- None of: fades, eases longer than 8 frames, drifting text
- None of: centred paragraphs; more than 9 words on screen
- All five signature moves from `style.md` appear somewhere in the film.
