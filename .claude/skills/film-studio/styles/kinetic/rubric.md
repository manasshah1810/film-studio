# Kinetic type: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Type as image | Type fills at least half the frame width and is the dominant shape. | Small type with empty space or illustration doing the work. |
| 2 | Word count | Nine words or fewer per screen; one idea. | Sentences or stacked paragraphs. |
| 3 | Slam and stop | Lines land in under 8 frames and are dead still while read. | Slow eases, drift or bounce. |
| 4 | Three-colour limit | No more than three colours visible in any panel. | A fourth colour or tints. |
| 5 | Boxed emphasis | Exactly one boxed word per panel and it is the word the voice stresses. | No emphasis, or several boxes. |
| 6 | Ghost glyph | Outlined, cropped by an edge, low contrast, tied to the key word. | Missing, uncropped, or louder than the headline. |
| 7 | Slab composition | The slab anchors the layout and its position varies across panels. | Slab absent or identical every panel. |
| 8 | Single display face | One display family for the whole film. | Mixed display faces. |
| 9 | Rhythm on the cut | Panel lengths vary with the sentence stress; cuts land on the beat. | Even metronome pacing. |
| 10 | Edge tension | Type is anchored to edges and corners, not centred. | Everything centred and symmetrical. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
