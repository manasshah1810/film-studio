# Blueprint: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sheet furniture | Grid, border and title block present and aligned. | Any missing, or content crossing the border. |
| 2 | Line constancy | One line width, butt caps, square corners. | Varying widths or round caps. |
| 3 | Hatch for mass | Mass shown by 45-degree hatch only. | Solid fills. |
| 4 | Dimensioning | Dimension line with ticks and arrowheads under the subject. | No dimension line, or decorative arrows. |
| 5 | Mono labelling | Kickers, attributions and chart labels in the mono face. | Labels in the display face. |
| 6 | Corner-tick figure | Key number counted up inside four corner ticks, with unit. | Boxed or unframed number, missing unit. |
| 7 | Orthographic view | No perspective, rotation or zoom. | Tilted or 3D elements. |
| 8 | Plotter motion | Lines draw at constant speed; text types. | Bounces or fades. |
| 9 | Grid alignment | Text blocks align to grid lines and a shared left edge. | Floating or centred blocks. |
| 10 | Accent economy | Primary for the one key value, secondary for dimensions; everything else ink. | Colour used decoratively. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
