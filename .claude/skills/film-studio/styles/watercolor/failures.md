# Watercolour: failure modes

Check these before sending stills to review. Each was seen in a proof render or follows directly from the kit.

1. **Body text sits on a dark wash rim.** Wash landed behind a text block. Change the film `seed` or the panel `anchor`; never raise wash opacity above 0.34.
2. **Looks like pastel flat design.** Only text panels used. Include figure or object panels so bleed and pencil are visible.
3. **Feels rushed.** Panels under 2 s cut the bloom. Keep min hold 45 frames.
4. **Charts look weak.** Thin line charts lack weight here. Use area type or convert to a stat or timeline.
5. **Script display face too thin at small sizes.** Pairings like tall-and-thin need `scale`; they carry it. Do not use them for headlines over 5 words.
6. **Colours turn muddy where washes overlap.** Three washes plus two cast colours. Keep cast to one accent colour per panel.

## Quick self-check on a still
- None of: dark or saturated backgrounds: the ground is always light paper
- None of: hard-edged flat fills with no bleed
- None of: pure black outlines thicker than 2 figure units
- None of: snaps, bounces, overshoots and hard cuts within a scene
- All five signature moves from `style.md` appear somewhere in the film.
