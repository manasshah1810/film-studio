# Watercolour: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Paper and grain | Light paper with visible tooth in every panel. | Flat digital background. |
| 2 | Wash quality | Three pools, rim darker than centre, soft irregular edges. | Hard blobs, even fills, or more than three. |
| 3 | Bleed past the line | Fill colour pools beyond the pencil outline; translucent. | Opaque fills that stop at the line. |
| 4 | Pencil line | Thin, loose, graphite-weight line under 2 units. | Heavy black outline or no line. |
| 5 | Text on clean paper | Body copy sits on unwashed or lightly washed paper and passes contrast. | Text over a wash rim. |
| 6 | Brushstroke emphasis | One highlight stroke under the hero line; puddle under the key number. | Boxes, underlines or digital highlights. |
| 7 | Grounding pool | Subjects sit on a pale ground wash. | Floating subjects. |
| 8 | Tempo | Slow blooms and dissolves; long holds. | Snaps, bounces or cuts inside a scene. |
| 9 | Palette restraint | Soft, natural colours; no neon; one cast accent per panel. | Saturated or muddy mixing. |
| 10 | Literary typography | Serif or script display with generous leading. | Heavy grotesk or tight display type. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
