# Cartoon: style rubric

Scored by the reviewers on proof stills and `film.json`, never by the author. Score each parameter 1-10.
Pass: average 9.0 or more from both reviewers and no parameter under 7 (see `rubrics/review-protocol.md`).

| # | Parameter | 9-10 looks like | 6 or below when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outline consistency | One ink outline weight on every shape, round joins, never coloured. | Mixed weights, missing outlines or coloured strokes. |
| 2 | Hard shadow | Props and big numerals carry a solid offset shadow with zero blur, same direction everywhere. | Soft shadows, or shadows missing on the hero element. |
| 3 | Grounding | Every figure stands on the ground band with a squash shadow. | Any figure floating or cropped at the feet. |
| 4 | Proportion and appeal | Heads at least a third of body height; silhouettes read at thumbnail size. | Realistic proportions or two characters that look the same. |
| 5 | Acting | Pose and expression match the line; two characters contrast. | Idle poses and neutral faces on a dramatic beat. |
| 6 | Sunburst restraint | Sky wedges stay low-contrast and peak only on the gag. | Wedges compete with text or peak on every panel. |
| 7 | Colour cast limit | Two character colours plus skin per panel, all from palette roles. | A third character colour or off-palette colour. |
| 8 | Bounce with landing | Entrances overshoot then settle and hold still while read. | Linear moves, or motion that never stops. |
| 9 | Type size | Nothing under 3.4 u; headline 2-6 words. | Small captions or paragraphs. |
| 10 | Gag timing | The one punch-in or starburst lands on the script's turn. | Emphasis effects spent on setup lines. |

Evidence: name the panel id for every score under 9. A score with no panel id is void.
