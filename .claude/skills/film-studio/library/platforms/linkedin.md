# Platform: linkedin

Professional feed video, watched at work, often muted. Numbers are from
third-party guides, not LinkedIn's spec [CHECK]; verify on desktop and mobile.

## Aspect ratio and safe zones
- Default 4:5 (1080x1350) for mobile feed; 1:1 (1080x1080) and 16:9
  (1920x1080) also fit [CHECK]. 9:16 works on mobile only [CHECK].
- Record the chosen ratio. Desktop may crop or letterbox 4:5 and 9:16 [CHECK].
- Keep text and faces inside a 5% margin [CHECK]. Keep the bottom 12% clear of
  the player bar and burned-in captions' overlap [CHECK]. No UI zones listed;
  9:16 in the vertical feed adds overlays [CHECK].

## Length range and sweet spot
- 30-90 s sweet spot; max about 10 min [CHECK]; the brief sets the length.

## Hook window
- Text hook on screen within 3 s: the insight or a sharp question [CHECK].
- Lead with the insight, not a pitch, greeting, logo or company intro.
- Frame 1 works muted and as a thumbnail.

## Pacing rules
- Steady: a visual change every 3-6 s [CHECK]; one idea per shot; no glitch.

## Caption rules
- Mandatory, burned in from frame 1: assume muted autoplay [CHECK].
- Max 2 lines, about 42 characters per line [CHECK], 4-8 words at a time,
  high contrast, inside the margin, never over the face or a chart.
- Spell numbers and names correctly; captions carry the full story.

## Retention techniques
- Open with the insight, then show the proof: example, number or short story.
- End on one takeaway or question that invites comments; no hard sell.

## Audio notes
- Must work fully muted. With sound: one clear human voice above light music.
- No stock hype music or sound-effect stings; no silence over 2 s.

## Do and do not
- Do: insight first, human tone, captions throughout, one takeaway.
- Do not: pitch in the first 10 s, jargon walls, clickbait, trend memes,
  logo opens, text in the bottom player zone.

## Checks for the Storyboard role
- Panel 1 shows a text hook within 3 s and is readable with no sound.
- Every caption is present, inside the margin and the safe area.
- Panels 1-2 state the insight; no pitch, greeting, logo or meme panel.
- Panel durations average 3-6 s; none over 8 without a reason.
- The engine places every slot inside the safe area set in `library/platforms.json`; confirm it on the proof sheet.
