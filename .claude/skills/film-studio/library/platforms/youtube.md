# Platform: youtube

Two formats. Ask the user, or infer: vertical, "short" or under a minute means
Shorts; 16:9 or over 3 minutes means long. Record the choice. Numbers are from
third-party guides, not YouTube's spec [CHECK]; verify in the app.

## Aspect ratio and safe zones
- Long: 16:9, 1920x1080; text inside a 5% margin [CHECK]. Keep the last 20 s
  free of key content for the end screen [CHECK].
- Shorts: 9:16, 1080x1920. Safe area about 1080x1350 centered [CHECK]; keep
  text and faces out of the top 180 px, right 120 px, bottom 20% [CHECK].

## Length range and sweet spot
- Shorts: under 60 s [CHECK]; app max 3 min since Oct 2024 [CHECK]; sweet
  spot 20-45 s [CHECK]. Long: 5-15 min [CHECK]; the brief sets the length.

## Hook window
- Shorts: within 2 s [CHECK]. Long: within 5-8 s, full hook by 30 s.
- Open on the payoff, movement or a question; no logo sting or greeting.
- Frame 1 works as a thumbnail: one focal point, big readable text.

## Pacing rules
- Long: pattern interrupt every 20-40 s [CHECK]: new angle, graphic or B-roll.
- Shorts: a new shot or visual change every 1-3 s [CHECK].

## Caption rules
- Shorts: burned in, 2 lines max, 3-6 words at a time, inside the safe area
  above the bottom zone [CHECK].
- Long: captions for key terms and quotes; lower third, inside the margin.

## Retention techniques
- Long: chapters with keyword titles, not "Part 1"; first chapter under 30 s
  [CHECK]. Open loops; recap before the outro.
- Shorts: loop-friendly ending; the last frame leads into the first.

## Audio notes
- Voice above music; no dead silence. Shorts must work muted.

## Do and do not
- Do: hook first, chapters (long), one focal point. Do not: slow intros,
  stings, text in overlay zones.

## Checks for the Storyboard role
- Format stated. Panel 1 is the hook and works as a thumbnail; it lands in
  2 s (Shorts) or 5-8 s (long).
- Shorts: all key content and captions sit inside the safe area.
- Long: image or angle changes every 40 s or less; a chapter panel per section;
  the last 20 s keeps empty end-screen space.
- The engine places every slot inside the safe area set in `library/platforms.json`; confirm it on the proof sheet.
