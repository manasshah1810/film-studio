# Platform: studying

Long-form learning video, not a feed. A content type, not one app; all
numbers are starting values [CHECK]. Recheck the host site's specs [CHECK].

## Aspect ratio and safe zones
- Default 16:9 (1920x1080); 9:16 only if the brief asks. No UI safe zones.
- Keep a 5% margin for text [CHECK]; with captions on, keep key content out of
  the bottom 12%.

## Length range and sweet spot
- Long-form 5-30 minutes, sweet spot 8-15 [CHECK]. Over 30: split into a series.

## Hook window
- First 15 seconds: state the learning outcome ("By the end you can ...") and
  one reason it matters. No logo sting or greeting first.

## Pacing rules
- Calm. A new idea or visual every 8-15 seconds [CHECK], not 3-5.
- One concept per scene; short pause after each key definition.

## Caption rules
- Optional but useful; always caption key terms.
- Max 2 lines of about 42 characters [CHECK], lower third, inside the margin;
  never cover diagrams or formulas.

## Retention techniques
- Chapters: a title card per section, at least one per 3 minutes.
- Key-term cards at first use: term plus one-line definition, 3-5 seconds.
- Recap every 3-5 minutes [CHECK]: three lines at most.
- Recall questions: ask, pause 3-5 seconds, then answer.

## Audio notes
- One clear voice, about 130-150 words per minute [CHECK]. Light music under
  it, none in recall pauses; quiet room tone, never dead silence.

## Do and do not
- Do: outcome early, define each term once, diagrams over decoration, end
  with a recap plus one question.
- Do not: fast cuts, shake, glitch transitions, heavy effects, or promise
  outcomes the video cannot deliver.

## Checks for the Storyboard role
- A panel in the first 15 seconds shows the outcome on screen.
- Each section opens with a chapter panel; a recap panel every 3-5 minutes.
- Every new script term has a key-term card panel.
- Recall panel holds 3-5 seconds before its answer panel.
- Captions sit in the lower third inside the margin; confirm it on the proof sheet.
- Panel durations average 8-15 seconds; none under 3 without a reason.
