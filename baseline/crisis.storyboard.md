# Storyboard: How the 2008 Financial Crisis Happened

Format: 1920x1080, 30 fps, 1800 frames. Component: `CrisisBaseline`.

Look: dark navy background with a faint grid, off-white type, amber for the boom, red for the bust, green only for "approved" ticks. Every scene has a step label top left, a progress bar along the top, and the narration as a caption at the bottom. Scenes cross-fade over 12 frames.

## Scene 1: Hook (frames 0-150, 5s)
- A jagged market line draws across the screen and plunges, in red.
- "2008" springs in very large, centre.
- Subtitle fades up: "The year the financial system almost broke."
- Then: "How did it happen?" in amber.

## Scene 2: Cheap money (150-420, 9s)
- Label: "STEP 1 - CHEAP MONEY".
- Left card: Fed interest rate counts down from 6.5% to 1.0%, labelled "2000 to 2003".
- Right: line chart of US home prices 2000-2006 draws from 100 to 184 in amber, with a moving end dot.
- Badge pops at the end of the line: "+84%".

## Scene 3: Risky loans (420-690, 9s)
- Label: "STEP 2 - RISKY LOANS".
- Left: three checklist lines appear one by one, each struck through: "Proof of income", "Down payment", "Good credit".
- Right: a 6x4 grid of house icons pops in one at a time, each getting a green "approved" tick.
- Bottom stat: subprime share of new mortgages counts from 8% to 20%.

## Scene 4: Securitisation (690-990, 10s)
- Label: "STEP 3 - PACKAGE AND SELL".
- Four nodes appear left to right: "Home loans", "Investment banks", "MBS and CDOs", "Global investors".
- Arrows draw between them and dots flow along the arrows.
- A red-bordered "AAA" stamp slams onto the third node.
- Closing line: "The risk did not disappear. It was passed along, and hidden."

## Scene 5: Leverage (990-1200, 7s)
- Label: "STEP 4 - BORROW BIG".
- A row of 31 blocks builds: 1 amber ("own money") and 30 grey ("borrowed").
- Headline: "30 to 1".
- A "-3%" marker hits: the amber block turns red and empties. Text: "A 3% loss and the bank's own money is gone."

## Scene 6: The bust (1200-1440, 8s)
- Label: "STEP 5 - THE BUBBLE BURSTS".
- The full 2000-2012 home price chart: the boom part is already drawn in amber, the fall from 184 to 134 draws in red.
- Badge: "-27%".
- Right: a row of houses flips from amber to red one by one with "FORECLOSED" underneath; the AAA stamp reappears and is replaced by "TOXIC".

## Scene 7: Panic (1440-1620, 6s)
- Label: "STEP 6 - PANIC".
- Five dominoes stand on a floor line, each labelled with a name and date: Bear Stearns, Fannie and Freddie, Lehman Brothers, AIG, WaMu. They topple left to right. Lehman is red.
- Headline: "September 15, 2008: Lehman Brothers goes bankrupt".
- Subline: "Banks stop lending to each other. Credit freezes."

## Scene 8: Aftermath and takeaway (1620-1800, 6s)
- Label: "THE AFTERMATH".
- Three stat cards count up: "$700B US bank bailout", "8.7M US jobs lost", "-54% Dow Jones, peak to trough".
- Final equation builds word by word: "Cheap debt + Hidden risk + Too much leverage = 2008".
- Fade to black over the last 15 frames.
