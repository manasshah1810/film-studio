# Why Do Cats Knock Things Off Tables? — Storyboard

1080x1920, 30fps, 900 frames. Flat vector style. Recurring set: a black cat with white paws sits on a cream table (left two thirds of frame, table top at y=1200, edge at x=760). Objects sit near the edge and fall off the right side. Captions live in the top third (below the TikTok top safe zone); nothing important in the bottom 350px or far right column.

## Scene 1 — Hook (frames 0-90, 3s) — yellow background
- Big title pops in word by word: "WHY do cats knock things off tables?"
- Cat stares straight at camera, extends paw (f30-40), slowly pushes a red mug to the edge (f40-62).
- Mug tips, falls, spins off-screen. "CRASH!" burst + screen shake at f86. Hard cut.

## Scene 2 — Reason 1: Hunter instinct (frames 90-270, 6s) — teal background
- Badge "1" + title "HUNTER INSTINCT", subtitle: Paws poke "prey" to check if it's alive.
- Object: toy mouse. Cat's pupils are wide, eyes track the toy.
- Two tentative taps (f50, f75): toy wobbles and scoots a little each time. "tap" labels pop up.
- Full push at f110, falls at f132, crash at f156.

## Scene 3 — Reason 2: Attention (frames 270-450, 6s) — coral background
- Badge "2" + title "IT WORKS ON YOU", subtitle: You gasp. You run over. Free attention.
- Object: glass of water. Cat looks at camera the whole time (it knows you're watching).
- Push at f70, falls at f92. Speech bubble from off-screen human: "NOOO!" (f112).
- Hearts float up from the smug cat after the human reacts.

## Scene 4 — Reason 3: Boredom / gravity research (frames 450-630, 6s) — purple background
- Badge "3" + title "GRAVITY RESEARCH", subtitle: Bored cats run experiments. Result: it falls. Every time.
- Object: potted plant. One test tap (f55), push at f85, falls at f107.
- Dotted trajectory arc draws from table edge downward with a "9.8 m/s²" label; cat watches it fall.

## Scene 5 — Recap + fix (frames 630-780, 5s) — light blue background
- Title "SO IT'S NOT SPITE".
- Three chips stagger in: "Instinct", "Attention", "Boredom".
- Tip card slides up: "Fix: more playtime, fewer things on the edge."
- Cat sits calmly on empty table, tail swishing, slow blink.

## Scene 6 — CTA (frames 780-900, 4s) — yellow background
