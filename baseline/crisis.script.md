# How the 2008 Financial Crisis Happened

60-second YouTube explainer. 1920x1080, 30 fps, 1800 frames.
The narration below is also shown as on-screen captions, so the video works without audio.

| # | Time | Frames | Narration |

## Figures used on screen

- Fed funds target rate: 6.5% (2000) to 1% (2003).
- US national home price index (Case-Shiller, 2000 = 100): about 184 at the 2006 peak, about 134 at the 2012 trough (roughly -27%).
- Subprime share of new mortgages: about 8% (2003) to about 20% (2006).
- Investment bank leverage: roughly 30 to 1 (Lehman Brothers, 2007).
- 2008 timeline: Bear Stearns rescue sale (March), Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac seized (Sep 7), Lehman bankruptcy (Sep 15), AIG rescue of $85B (Sep 16), Washington Mutual failure (Sep 25).
- TARP bailout: $700 billion authorised. US jobs lost: about 8.7 million. Dow Jones peak to trough: about -54%.
