# Why Nokia Failed — Storyboard

**Canvas:** 1080x1920, 30 fps, 1350 frames. Component: `NokiaBaseline`.
**Look:** Deep navy background with a slow drifting grid, electric blue accent, white heavy sans-serif type. The background shifts toward dark red during the collapse and returns to navy for the lesson.
**Safe zones:** Key content stays between y=250 and y=1560 to clear the Reels top bar and bottom caption/UI area.
**Persistent elements:** Thin progress bar at the top; every scene fades in and out over 8 frames.
**Motion language:** Words spring up one by one; cards slide and scale in; numbers count up.
