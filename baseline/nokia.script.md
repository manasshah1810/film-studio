## Fact notes

- About 40% of global handset sales and about 49% of smartphone sales at the 2007 peak (analyst estimates).
- World's largest phone vendor from 1998 to 2012 (14 years).
- Nokia 1100: over 250 million units sold.
- February 2011: "burning platform" memo, then the Windows Phone partnership.
- Smartphone share figures in the chart are approximate yearly estimates (49, 44, 39, 33, 16, 5, 3) and are labelled as approximate on screen.
- September 2013: devices business sale to Microsoft announced, about EUR 5.4 billion.
- The "fear" point follows the well-known INSEAD study of Nokia managers (Vuori and Huy).
