# Script iterations: How did OneCoin convince millions?

Raw prompt only: "How Did OneCoin Scam Millions of People? ... how did OneCoin convince millions of people to
invest in a cryptocurrency that wasn't even real?" No style, length or script hints were given.
Director lock: archival-grunge / true-crime / abril-courier-caveat, runtime tier 60-90 (target 72 s), voiced.

## Iteration 1: multi-angle discovery and causal spine (angles.json + Draft v1)
Eight angles mined into `angles.json`, scored 1 to 10 on Curiosity ("wait, what?!"), Showability and Stakes:

Selected (top 4 totals): a1, a5, a3, a4. Cut as filler or unsourced: a2 (folded into the hook as stakes,
no figure on screen), a8, a7, a6 (a second court story dilutes the one question).

Draft v1 (causal skeleton, therefore / but / until):
1. OneCoin raised over four billion dollars as a cryptocurrency, THEREFORE people assumed real technology.
2. BUT prosecutors say there was no real blockchain; coins were handed out from nowhere.
3. THEREFORE something else had to sell it: Ruja Ignatova on stage at Wembley.
4. BUT a stage reaches one arena, not millions; the commission plan did the rest.
5. UNTIL October 2017, when she flew to Athens and vanished.
6. THEREFORE the answer: it sold belief, and paid believers to recruit.
7. Close: FBI Ten Most Wanted; she was never found, neither was the coin.

## Iteration 2: hard question hook and re-hooks (Draft v2)
- v1 opened on a statement ("OneCoin raised over four billion dollars"), which is a title. Rewritten as a
  question that challenges the viewer's assumption that nobody falls for an imaginary coin:
  **"Would you hand your savings to a coin that never existed?"** (11 words, lands inside 4 s), answered
  at once by the stakes: "Millions of people did, and OneCoin raised over four billion dollars."
- Re-hook plan (each opens a new loop, every 12 to 15 s at most):
  - ~14 s "But OneCoin kept its ledger private..." (loop: then what were people buying?)
  - ~25 s "Yet millions believed, because..." (loop: who made them believe?)
  - ~35 s "But a speech alone cannot reach millions..." (loop: how did belief spread?)
  - ~45 s "Until October 2017..." (loop: where did she go?)
- The hook gap: "If the coin was never real, what were millions of people actually buying?"

## Iteration 3: the 5-second micro-explainer and cold-viewer clarity (Draft v3)
- The twist ("no real blockchain") only lands if a cold viewer knows what a blockchain does. Inserted ONE
  micro-explainer at ~8 s, before the twist, about 6.5 s long, marked `explainer: true`:
  **"A real crypto coin lives on a public ledger: thousands of copies, so nobody can fake a balance."**
  Picture: one ledger page fans out onto a wall of identical copies; a forged row flashes red on every copy.
- Jargon removed: "mining", "nodes", "MLM", "Ponzi" (replaced by "commission for each friend").
- Pronoun audit: v2 scene 4 said "she filled Wembley" before any person was named, and scene 6 said "she
  flew to Athens" with no anchor. Fixed: "Dr Ruja Ignatova" is named in full at her first mention (s4);
  "Ruja" carries it after. "its bosses" is anchored to "OneCoin" in the same sentence.

## Iteration 4: hype cadence, power words and dramatic pauses (Draft v4)
- Flat line "OneCoin was promoted with a multi-level marketing structure" rewritten as storytelling:
  "Every buyer earned a commission for each friend they signed up." then the drop line
  "The real product was the next recruit."
- Build-up / drop alternation: long setup lines (14 to 19 words) against punch drops of 3 to 7 words
  ("Neither was the coin." / "and vanished.").
- Pauses placed right before each major reveal (punch after silence):
  - s3: "But OneCoin kept its ledger private. [PAUSE 0.9s] Prosecutors say there was no real blockchain..."
  - s5: "...for each friend they signed up. [PAUSE 0.6s] The real product was the next recruit."
  - s6: "...from Sofia to Athens, [PAUSE 0.9s] and vanished."
  - s8: "She was never found. [PAUSE 0.6s] Neither was the coin."
  The reveal scenes s3, s5, s6 are marked `impact: true` for the sub-bass hit.
- Zero duplicate screen copy: headline vs caption pairs checked. "PUBLIC LEDGER" was also spoken, so the
  headline became "EVERYONE HOLDS A COPY"; "TEN MOST WANTED" was spoken, so the headline became
  "WANTED BY THE FBI". Captions are 2 to 4 words per chunk.

## Iteration 5: adversarial retention audit and visual-metaphor lock (final script.json)
Critique of Draft v4 as a bored scroller (1 to 10): scroll-stop 8, tension 7, pacing 7, showability 8.
- Weak spot 1 (pacing 7): s4 and s5 were both "explanation" beats in a row. Fix: s5 now opens on a
  challenge, "But a speech alone cannot reach millions." so it is a re-hook, and it is the film's one turn.
- Weak spot 2 (tension 7): the payoff restated facts. Fix: the payoff answers the hook gap in one line,
  "So OneCoin never needed a blockchain. It needed believers, and it paid them to find more."
- Weak spot 3 (scroll-stop 8): the close originally summarised. Fix: the close loops to the hook's
  "coin that never existed" with "Neither was the coin." after a pause.
- Transition audit: hook -> explainer (SO you know what real looks like) -> BUT private -> YET believed ->
  BUT a speech cannot scale -> UNTIL she vanished -> SO the answer -> close. No "and then" link remains.
- visualMetaphor locked for every beat, all distinct, close unique (never the opening coin):
  - s1 a glowing gold coin turns edge-on and is revealed as a paper-thin cutout with nothing behind it.
  - s2 one ledger page fans out into a wall of identical copies; a forged row flashes red on every copy.
  - s3 the wall of copies goes dark; one locked private notebook remains and a hand types balances in.
  - s4 arena spotlights sweep and lock on a podium while thousands of phone lights rise in the crowd.
  - s5 a recruitment tree grows downward; money climbs every branch to a single crown at the top.
  - s6 a boarding pass tears in two as a flight line draws Sofia to Athens and the radar blip fades.
  - s7 a pair of scales: a heavy pile of signed believers outweighs an empty blockchain block.
  - s8 a case file slides in, a WANTED stamp lands, then an empty coin outline is stamped NOT FOUND.
- Re-scored after fixes: scroll-stop 9, tension 9, pacing 9, showability 9. script.json written.

## Review round 1 (Producer 7.6, Director-review 7.2: both under the 9.0 gate)

- Fact discipline (4 and 5): "millions" could not be sourced (justice.gov unreachable from the build sandbox),
  so it is gone from s1, s4 and s5; the hook now rests on the verified $4 billion. "filled Wembley" became
  "took the stage" (the source's words), "Dr" and "thousands of copies" were cut, "every buyer... each friend"

  fact dated to the January 2023 report. Headline "ZERO BLOCKCHAIN" became "CLOSED BOOKS".

  payoff (s6) follows the turn (s5) directly, and the flight moved into the close, which now lands the
  recruitment idea: "Her recruits were left holding a coin that never existed." (loops to the hook).
- One product: s5 says "The coin was the excuse. The recruit was the product."; the payoff headline is
  "BELIEF ON COMMISSION", so the film names one answer.
- Hook (8): "Four billion dollars for a coin that never existed?" names the stakes in the first 2 s; OneCoin
  is named in the next line. The stock "Would you..." opener is gone.
- Voice (7-8): scene openers are no longer But / Yet / But / Until / So in a row (now: OneCoin..., So who...,

- Visual intent: s4 and the close state the founder is never drawn (empty podium, a boarding pass).
- s6 metaphor replaced the stock scales: a relay of hands passing a coin, each taking a commission slip,
